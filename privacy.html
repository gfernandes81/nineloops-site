Nine Loops privacy policy

Português

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Nine Loops is a puzzle game for Android and iOS. This page explains which data the game uses.

Summary

  • Nine Loops does not collect personal data.
  • The game works offline and sends no data to any server.
  • There are no ads, no purchases, no accounts and no tracking.

Data kept on your device

The game keeps, on the device itself:

  • your progress: the levels you won, your fewest rotations and the stars of each level;
  • the level in progress, so that you can continue it later;
  • your settings: sound, music, vibration, reduced motion and language.

This data stays in the app's storage (UserDefaults on iOS and SharedPreferences on Android) and never leaves the device. To erase it, use Settings → Erase progress or uninstall the game.

Permissions

The game does not ask for access to the camera, the microphone, your location, contacts, photos or any other data on the device.

Crash reports from the stores

If you allowed it in your device settings, Apple and Google may send the developer anonymous crash reports about the app. These reports follow Apple's and Google's privacy policies. The game uses no other reporting or analytics service.

Children and teenagers

Nine Loops is rated for ages 9 and up and may be played by children. Because the game collects no personal data, it processes no data about children or teenagers (Brazil's General Data Protection Law, LGPD, article 14, and Law 15.211/2025, the "ECA Digital").

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the LGPD give you rights over your personal data. Because Nine Loops processes no personal data, the developer keeps no data about you. The game's data stays only on your device, under your control.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it uses data, for example with an online leaderboard, this policy will be updated before that version, with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, see the support page.
