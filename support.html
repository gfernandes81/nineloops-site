How to play

  • Tap the left half of a ring to turn it counter-clockwise, and the right half to turn it clockwise.
  • Tap a bed to drop the ball from the chute into it. Every row, column and room gets one cat of each kind.
  • Two cats that dislike each other never sleep side by side: not above, below or beside each other. Diagonally is fine.
  • A ball that has no place yet can wait in the basket.
  • Each level gives up to three stars: win within par rotations, win without the basket, and win without undo.

Frequently asked questions

I lost my progress. Your progress is kept only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing the app's data erases it, and the game has no cloud backup.

How do I start over? Use Settings → Erase progress. Your settings stay as they are.

Does the game need the internet? No.

Are there ads or purchases? No.

What data does the game collect? None. See the privacy policy.
